## Phase Goal

**As a** Kitcha user planning meals on my phone, **I want to** get recipe, meal-plan and substitution suggestions and product lookups that are always well-formed, respect my diet, and tell me clearly when AI is unavailable or I hit my daily limit, **so that** I can trust the suggestions without errors and without the app costing money.

<objective>
Slice 1: recipe suggestions work end to end through ONE env-configured OpenAI-compatible provider (default Dahl, model `MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M2.7`), with tolerant JSON extraction (MiniMax `<think>` output), Zod validation with exactly one repair retry, sanitized delimited prompts, and a clear "unavailable" error contract. A missing key never breaks startup or /health.

Purpose: replaces the Gemini-hard-wired, regex-parsed recipe path with the trusted pipeline every later plan builds on (cache/quota in 02-04, other features in 02-03).
Output: provider adapter, json-extract, orchestrator, error contract, recipes feature module, env vars for the whole phase, tests.

Note: suggest-substitutions and generate-meal-plan keep calling the old Gemini `AIService` until plan 02-03 migrates them and deletes it. Do NOT remove `GEMINI_AI_API_KEY`, `@google/generative-ai` or `src/modules/ai/ai.service.ts` in this plan (02-03 owns that).
</objective>
