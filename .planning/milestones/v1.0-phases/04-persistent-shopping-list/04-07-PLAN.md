## Phase Goal

**As a** Kitcha user shopping with my phone, **I want to** keep one saved shopping list that I can build by hand or from a meal plan, tick off in the aisle and close with what I actually spent, **so that** I buy what I need without losing the list or overspending.

<objective>

Purpose: SHOP-03 user-facing; per CONTEXT resolved decision "The Meal Plans page preview/detail gets an 'Add to my list' button that calls the new generate endpoint".
Output: pure summary helper + test, hook, component, two page edits.
</objective>
