## Phase Goal

**As a** Kitcha user shopping with my phone, **I want to** keep one saved shopping list that I can build by hand or from a meal plan, tick off in the aisle and close with what I actually spent, **so that** I buy what I need without losing the list or overspending.

<objective>
Pure, Vitest-covered frontend logic the list page renders from: one shared vocabulary aligned to the backend enums, category grouping with checked items sinking, estimated-vs-actual totals, and strict item input parsing.

Purpose: SHOP-04 grouping and SHOP-05 totals are pure derived views; putting them in `frontend/lib` makes them testable (Vitest only runs `lib/**/*.test.ts` in node env). Fixes the vocabulary mismatch (frontend constants have 7 categories and `pcs`-style units).
Output: four helper modules with tests.
</objective>
