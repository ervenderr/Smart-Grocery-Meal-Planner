  <how-to-verify>
    Phone (iPhone Safari 16.4+ or Android Chrome; note the device, OS and whether it was a browser tab or home-screen app):
    1. Log in, open Shopping. Quick-add "Milk" (Enter), "Chicken breast", "Bananas". Expect them under Dairy, Protein and Fruit sections, Other last.
    2. On a second device or a desktop browser logged in as the same user, open Shopping: the same three items appear. Check "Milk" there; back on the phone, switch away and back to the tab: Milk shows checked (refetch on focus).
    3. Open "Add from a meal plan" and add one of your meal plans: a toast reports items added/merged and the items appear grouped. Add the same plan again: quantities increase (merged), no duplicate lines.

    6. In Shopping mode, tick items with one thumb while holding the phone: every row is easy to hit (tapping anywhere on the row checks it), checked items move to the bottom of their section, and the summary bar stays visible above the bottom nav.
    7. Tap Finish shopping, keep the default "Keep unchecked items on a new list", confirm. Expect a "Trip saved" toast, unchecked items remain on a fresh list, and the trip appears under Past trips with its total.
