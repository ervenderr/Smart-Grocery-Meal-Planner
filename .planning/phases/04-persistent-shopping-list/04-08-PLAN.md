## Phase Goal

**As a** Kitcha user shopping with my phone, **I want to** keep one saved shopping list that I can build by hand or from a meal plan, tick off in the aisle and close with what I actually spent, **so that** I buy what I need without losing the list or overspending.

<objective>
Backend slice for closing a trip: "Finish shopping" with carry-over or discard of unchecked items, a ShoppingHistory row with the int4-safe total, and a paginated history endpoint.

Purpose: SHOP-05 (estimated vs actual spend) persisted beyond the trip; history rows also feed the existing analytics/alert aggregates of `shoppingHistory.totalPhpCents`.
Output: pure totals helper, finish/history service, endpoints, tests.
</objective>
