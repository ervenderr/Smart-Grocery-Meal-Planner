## Phase Goal

**As a** Kitcha user planning meals on my phone, **I want to** get recipe, meal-plan and substitution suggestions and product lookups that are always well-formed, respect my diet, and tell me clearly when AI is unavailable or I hit my daily limit, **so that** I can trust the suggestions without errors and without the app costing money.

<objective>
Slice: repeat requests are instant and free, and daily caps protect the free tier. Add Postgres-backed `AiCache` and `AiUsage` (additive migration applied by the entrypoint on deploy), a versioned cache key, an atomic per-user (20) and global (100) UTC-day quota, and wire them into runAiFeature: cache hit -> no quota; miss -> reserve -> provider -> validate -> cache -> refund on failure (per CONTEXT quotas and caching decisions).

Purpose: AI-03/AI-04 and the refund half of AI-05 graceful degradation. Applies automatically to all three features because they call runAiFeature.
Output: Prisma models + migration, cache-key, two repositories, orchestrator wiring, integration tests.
</objective>
