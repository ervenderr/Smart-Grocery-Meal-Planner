## Phase Goal

**As a** Kitcha user planning meals on my phone, **I want to** get recipe, meal-plan and substitution suggestions and product lookups that are always well-formed, respect my diet, and tell me clearly when AI is unavailable or I hit my daily limit, **so that** I can trust the suggestions without errors and without the app costing money.

<objective>
Slice: diet safety engine and fair burst limiting. Build the pure dietary/allergen filter (wired into all three features by 02-03) and replace the 10/hour AI limiter with a 10/minute per-user burst limiter that uses `ipKeyGenerator` (fixes `ERR_ERL_KEY_GEN_IPV6`, per CONTEXT). The daily cap becomes the DB quota (02-04).

Purpose: suggestions must never show items that violate the user's settings, enforced in code (CONTEXT: restrictions are never sent to the provider, only applied to output). The old hourly limiter would block free cache hits.
Output: dietary-filter.ts + table-driven tests; rateLimiter.ts + ai.routes.ts change + limiter test.
</objective>

New contract created here (consumed by 02-03):
- `normalizeRestrictions(...lists: ReadonlyArray<readonly string[] | null | undefined>): readonly string[]` lowercases, trims, collapses whitespace, dedupes, sorts, drops empties.
- `buildForbiddenTerms(restrictions: readonly string[]): readonly string[]` maps known tags to keyword sets and unknown free text to its meaningful tokens.
- `violates(texts: readonly string[], terms: readonly string[]): boolean` whole-word, case-insensitive match (plural 's'/'es' tolerated).
- `partitionByRestrictions<T>(items: readonly T[], getTexts: (item: T) => readonly string[], restrictions: readonly string[]): {kept: readonly T[]; removedCount: number}` returns new arrays, never mutates.
